What it is

It's a picnic table, built the way a picnic table is built — the top slats sit proud with gaps between them, the benches are held on their own legs, and there's an A-brace underneath doing the work rather than a block of glue.

You put corn or a cob on the top. The squirrels sit at the table for it, which is the entire point, and it is funnier every single time.

It sits flat on a rail, a stump, a deck post or a table. No hanging, no mounting, nothing to fix to anything.
