Privacy.

The short version: I collect what I need to build your piece and get it to you, and nothing else. I never share your details with anyone outside this shop, there isn't a single cookie on this website, and I don't track you. I may email you now and then about new work — and one reply stops it for good.

Last updated: July 31, 2026
In one paragraph: if you fill in a form, I get your name and how to reach you, and I use it to answer you. I may also use that address to tell you about new work now and then — one reply stops it. Your details never go to anybody outside this shop. Your cart is saved on your own computer, not mine. There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers on this site — not "limited" ones, none.

Who I am

I'm Harley Ritchie. I build red cedar swings, feeders, birdhouses, engraved signs and custom furniture by hand in Owingsville, Kentucky, trading as Harley's Handmade, LLC. This website is mine, and I'm the person who reads what you send.

You can reach me any time at harley@harleyshandmadeky.com or (859) 749-2814.

What I collect, and only when you give it to me

When you send me a message or an order

The contact form asks for your name, your email, your phone number if you want to give it, what you're after, and any details or dates you tell me. If you draw something on the sketch pad, that drawing comes with it. If you send an order from the cart, the list of what you picked comes with it.

I use that to answer you, price the work, build the piece and get it to you. That's all.

If you ask to hear about new pieces

There's a place on the shop page to leave your email so I can tell you when something new comes off the bench. That one is entirely your choice — nothing signs you up automatically.

And plainly, because you should hear it from me rather than work it out later: if you give me your email anywhere on this site — the signup, an order, the cart, the contact form — I may also use it to email you about my own work — a new piece, a batch just off the bench, or a deal I'm running. That is the only thing I'll ever email you for, and it only ever comes from me. Not often, and never a daily thing — I'm one man in a shop, not a mailing machine.

Every one of those has a way out, and one word does it. Reply and say stop, or write to harley@harleyshandmadeky.com, and you're off it. No form, no logging in, no waiting. I'll take you off within a few days at the very outside, and it doesn't touch an order you've already placed — I'll still write to you about that.

If you're on it, I'll only write when there's actually something new. Reply and say stop, and you're off it — no form to fill in, no waiting.

If you ask me to hold your cart

If you've picked something out but aren't ready, you can leave me your email and I'll hold it and answer questions. That address is for that conversation first — and, as above, it may also get the occasional note about new work. I won't pester you, and one reply takes you off.

What stays on your own computer

A few things are saved by your own browser, on your own device, and are never sent to me or to anyone else:

  • What's in your cart, so it's still there if you come back
  • The order text, if you send one through to the contact page
  • Whether you've dismissed the little "you left something in your cart" note
  • Whether you chose the guided walk on the home page

You can wipe all of it by clearing your browser's site data. Nothing breaks if you do.

What I don't do

  • No cookies. None at all.
  • No analytics. I don't know how many people visited this site or which pages they read.
  • No advertising trackers, no pixels, no retargeting, nothing from any ad network.
  • I don't sell, rent, trade or share your details with anybody outside this shop. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to another business, not for their marketing, not for anything, not ever. The only outsiders who touch your information at all are the few services listed below that carry a message or take a card — and they only get what they need for that one job.
  • I don't email anybody who never gave me their address in the first place. No bought lists, no scraped addresses.
  • I don't ask for anything I don't need. There's no account to create and no password to remember.

Who else touches it

I'm one person in a shop, so a few services do jobs I can't do myself. They only get what's needed for their part:

  • The form service that delivers your message to my inbox. Your message passes through it on the way to me.
  • My payment provider, once card payments are switched on. They handle the card, not me — I never see your card number, and that's deliberate. Their own privacy terms cover the payment itself.
  • The company that hosts this website. Like every website, their servers see the ordinary technical details of a request.
  • My email provider, because that's where your message lands.

Nobody on that list is given your details for their own marketing.

How long I keep it

Order and enquiry emails I keep as long as I need them for the job, my records and my taxes. Mailing-list addresses I keep until you tell me to stop. Ask me to delete something and I will — email or call and say so.

What you can ask me for

  • What I've got about you
  • A correction, if something's wrong
  • To be deleted, or taken off the list

No process and no forms. Just ask me and I'll do it.

Children

This is a woodworking shop, not a site aimed at children, and I don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13. If that's happened by accident, tell me and I'll delete it.

If this page changes

If I start doing something new with your details, this page gets updated and the date at the top changes. I'm not going to quietly widen it.

Questions about any of this? Call or text me on (859) 749-2814, or email harley@harleyshandmadeky.com. You'll get me, not somebody taking a message.
