Where I ship

I ship to the lower 48 states. I don't ship to Alaska or Hawaii, or outside the United States. The rate is the same whether you're in Kentucky or California.

Kentucky sales tax of 6% is added at checkout, on the pieces and on the shipping.

Per item, not per order

Each piece is packed on its own, so two pieces is two lots of postage. The cart adds it up in front of you before you pay.

The swing stand ships as its own package. It won't fit in the box with the swing, so a swing with a stand is $10 plus $8.

Turnaround

Shop pieces are ready to ship in 3 to 5 business days. Same for pickup: order at least 3 days ahead and pick your time at checkout.

Custom orders go piece by piece. How long one takes depends on what it is, and I'll give you an honest estimate when I quote it. See custom work.

Once it's with the carrier, how fast it moves is up to them.

Custom work

Anything built to your own measurements or design is quoted with the shipping included, worked out on the size and finished weight of the piece. You see that number before anything is cut.

Free local pickup

Free pickup at the Marathon station next to Ace Hardware in Owingsville. Order at least 3 days ahead and pick a time at checkout. If you're in Winchester, Mt. Sterling or Morehead, message me instead and I'll meet you, no charge.

If it arrives damaged or goes missing

Tell me and I'll help sort it out. The details are in the terms.
