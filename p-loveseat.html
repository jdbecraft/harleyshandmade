A note on Weathered Gray: it takes differently on every board. How gray it goes depends on the density of the wood, the species and the grain pattern — so the swatch shows you the finish, not an exact match for the piece I build you. On some boards it pulls soft and silvery, on others it stays warmer. That is the wood doing it, not the stain failing.

These are the finishes I keep on hand. If you want a stain I do not carry, or a painted finish, that makes it a custom piece — I would rather quote that properly than guess at it, so send me a message and I will price it fairly.

What it is

Same build as the porch swing, different shape. The back is scalloped — two dips cut across the top instead of a straight run — and it's wider, the loveseat rather than the single seat.

It's the one I'd pick for a feeder, because it holds more and the wider back keeps the weather off it. It hangs from rope the same way, and it takes the same stand.

Cedar throughout, sanded smooth, no sharp edges anywhere a hand or a bird is going to land.
