Bench to box

How a piece gets built.

01

Drawn

Lengths, thicknesses and hardware worked out on paper. Cheaper to fix a drawing than a board.

02

Cut

Stock milled to size. Cedar mostly — it holds up outside, which is where most of what I make ends up.

03

Fitted dry

Everything goes together once without glue, so I can see it wrong before it's permanent.

04

Glued and fastened

Clamped up and left alone. This is the part you can't rush, and the part most people do.

05

Sanded

By hand, through the grits, until the edges feel right rather than just look right.

06

Finished

Base cedar, stained cedar, cherry, walnut or weathered gray — or a stain or paint color you pick, which is a custom finish and carries a slight upcharge.

The finishes

Four standard, or whatever you want.

The five I keep on hand are base cedar, stained cedar, cherry, walnut and weathered gray. Most people pick from those. Anything outside them is a custom finish — I can do it, there's a slight upcharge, and you get the number first.

If none of them suit what it's going next to, you can pick a stain or a paint color instead and I'll match it. Costs the same.

Everything that lives outdoors gets sealed for weather. If you tell me where a piece is going — full sun, a covered porch, under a tree — I'll finish it for that.
