Handmade means each one is a bit different

Every piece is cut, built and finished by hand out of real cedar. Grain, colour and knots vary from board to board, and the same stain sits differently on different wood. The photographs on this site are of pieces I actually built, so yours will look like them — but it will not be an identical copy, and small differences in grain, tone and marking are normal and expected, not defects.

If you want something as close as possible to a particular photo, say so and I'll pick the board with that in mind.

Prices, and what makes something custom

Things listed in the shop

The prices on the shop page are what those pieces cost, in US dollars. Options that add to the price — a finish, a matching stand — are shown with what they add before you add anything to your cart.

Custom work

A piece becomes custom when it's built to your own measurements, your own design, a stain I don't carry, a painted finish, or when it's engraved. Custom work is always quoted before I start, and you see the number before anything is cut.

My custom rate is $45 an hour plus materials at cost plus 25%, with half down to get on the bench. If the job changes along the way, I tell you what that does to the price before I carry on.

Because a custom piece is drawn, measured and cut for you specifically, that time and material is spent whether the order goes ahead or not — which is what the deposit is for. I'd rather say that up front than bury it.

Engraving

Laser engraving is quoted on the engraving itself. A name is not the same job as a scene, so there's no flat price for it. Send me the wording or the artwork and I'll price it properly.

Sales tax

Kentucky sales tax is added where the law requires it. Where tax applies, it's shown before you pay — it's never folded into the price without being named.

Paying

Card payments are handled by my payment provider, not by me. I never see your card number. For larger custom work I'll usually send an invoice, and a bank transfer costs us both less than a card does.

An order isn't confirmed until the payment has actually gone through, or until I've said in writing that I'm starting on it.

Making it and getting it to you

How long

Pieces listed in the shop are ones I keep on hand or have built and waiting on finish, so you're waiting on me to sand, stain and box — not weeks. Custom work depends entirely on what it is, and I'll give you an honest estimate when I quote. If something slips, I'll tell you rather than let you wonder.

Shipping

Flat-rate shipping covers everything listed on the shop page, added at checkout, with nothing padded. Custom pieces are quoted with the shipping included, worked out on the size and finished weight of the piece I build you.

Once it's with the carrier, how fast it moves is up to them. If it goes missing or arrives damaged, that's still my problem to help with — see below.

Local pickup

Pickup is always free in Owingsville, Winchester, Mt. Sterling and Morehead. Most folks round here just meet me.

If something isn't right

Tell me. Call or email within a reasonable time of it arriving, send me a photo, and I'll sort it out — repair, replace or refund, whichever actually fixes it.

That covers something that arrives broken, something built wrong, or something that isn't what we agreed. It does not cover normal variation in the wood, ordinary wear, weather damage on a piece left out in it, or a change of mind on a custom piece made to your own words — because nobody else can use a sign with your family's name on it.

Looking after it

These are real wood, finished by hand. Left outside in Kentucky weather, cedar will grey and a finish will fade — that's the wood doing what wood does, not a fault. Bring it in over the worst of the winter and it'll last a very long time.

The photographs and the words on this site

The photographs are of my own work and the designs are mine. Please don't copy them to sell your own version. If you'd like to share a photo of a piece I made you, I'd be glad of it.

The honest limits

I'll do what I said I'd do and I'll stand behind my work. What I can't do is take responsibility for things outside my hands — a carrier losing a parcel, a piece used for something it wasn't built for, or weather. Nothing here takes away rights the law gives you as a consumer in Kentucky.

Which law applies

These terms are governed by the law of the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
