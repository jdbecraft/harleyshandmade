What it is

Solid black walnut plaque, hand-picked straight from the mill and finished in my shop in Owingsville, Kentucky.

The boot, decanter and lettering are all burned into the wood — no paint, no vinyl, no stickers, nothing to peel off in three months. Sanded smooth and finished with two coats of polyurethane.

Comes with a hanger already on the back, so it's ready to go up on a nail. Leans nicely on a shelf or mantel too. About 10.5" tall and 8" wide.

Black walnut runs anywhere from deep chocolate to a pale cream stripe where the sapwood comes through, and every board is different. Yours won't look exactly like the photo, and that's the point of buying handmade.

Stain available upon request — message me for a quote.
